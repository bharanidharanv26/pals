## Geographic interpretation

The current application catalogue contains 28 states and 8 union territories. The boundary packages retain their historical reference dates and may differ from current administrative boundaries. District-to-region grouping is derived by spatial intersection with the reference state layer, with a nearest-anchor fallback where required. The index's source identifiers are geoBoundaries identifiers, not newly verified current LGD district codes.

The national map and district index do not imply that all roads or facilities in those areas have been verified. Detailed routing is limited to the loaded package. All demo hospitals and their advertised capabilities are synthetic, even where their city names are real.

The optional street basemap uses normal interactive OSM tile requests with attribution. No OSM tile prefetch or bulk offline cache is included. Offline operation uses the locally distributed vector geometry and road packages.

## Public-feed interpretation

USGS observations are bounded by latitude/longitude and include neighbouring countries. They are not statements of casualties or incidents inside a particular Indian district. The feed panel preserves source and retrieval time, caches requests for 15 minutes and reports network failures.

SACHET is configurable because its current published feed endpoint must be checked against its integration guide. Its adapter retains ETags, sends `If-None-Match`, handles 304 responses and keeps cached source timestamps. An unavailable feed is never replaced with invented live warnings.

CAP imports preserve `Actual` / `Exercise` / `Test` status and the document's issue/expiry fields. Imported text remains source content. Public observations and imported warnings do not alter a simulation unless the operator creates a separate scenario incident.
